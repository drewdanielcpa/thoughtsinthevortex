500 Down, 556 to Go

DNA Deep Dive, Part 2 of 2

After the tie DNA came back with no CODIS hits, Seattle set out to review all 1,057 suspect subfiles opened since 1971 and pick the best men for direct DNA testing. In early 2004 one agent graded folders 1–500 by hand on a 22-page "Work Copy," circled nine entries, and sent five men to DNA. Then he drew a line under #500 and handed the project off with orders to review the remaining 556 folders "as soon as possible." As far as the released files show, that second half was never done. Part 1 covers the biological evidence and the lab testing.

Key findings

  • Two copies, 212 names restored. Pairing the heavily censored 2004 Work Copy (Part 90) with a cleaner 1999 copy of the same list (Part 106) restores 212 of the first 514 folder titles.
  • 501 graded → 9 circles → 5 DNA picks. Peterson, Langseth and Catalano were circled with no elimination mark; McCoy and Weber were added to reach five. Celk was circled but dropped, and "Pull Letters for DNA" was never acted on.
  • The dropped handoff. Reassigned on August 4, 2004 with orders to review the other 556 folders, including Rackstraw, List, the LaCenter pay phone and the ransom-serial $20 bill, plus 355 still-censored folders.
