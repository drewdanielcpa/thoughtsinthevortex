Splitting Hair(s) Slides

DNA Deep Dive, Part 1 of 2

Every biological item the FBI took off Flight 305 and what became of it: the eight Raleigh cigarette butts destroyed in Las Vegas, the Seat 18-E headrest hair slide last returned to Las Vegas in 1984, and the clip-on tie that gave up a 9-locus DNA mixture in 2002. The report follows how the Lab's reading of that mixture changed through 2011, every suspect compared against it, and what sits in the 58 pages of unreleased bench notes. Part 2 covers the 2004 subfile audit that picked the first suspects.

About the write-ups

Everything here comes from the FBI's own NORJAK file as released on the FBI Vault, plus clearly marked outside sources. A citation like 110 / 267 / 566 means Vault Part 110, PDF page 267, Bates stamp DB Cooper-566, so you can open the page and read it yourself.

Each report comes with its sources: the Elsinore Ghost and Oscillations reports have companion PDFs reproducing every FBI page they cite, each page labeled with its Part, PDF page and Bates number.
