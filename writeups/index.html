500 Down, 556 to Go

DNA Deep Dive, Part 2 of 2

After the tie DNA came back with no CODIS hits, Seattle set out to review all 1,057 suspect subfiles opened since 1971 and pick the best men for direct DNA testing. In early 2004 one agent graded folders 1–500 by hand on a 22-page "Work Copy," circled nine entries, and sent five men to DNA. Then he drew a line under #500 and handed the project off with orders to review the remaining 556 folders "as soon as possible." As far as the released files show, that second half was never done. Part 1 covers the biological evidence and the lab testing.

About the write-ups

Everything here comes from the FBI's own NORJAK file as released on the FBI Vault, plus clearly marked outside sources. A citation like 110 / 267 / 566 means Vault Part 110, PDF page 267, Bates stamp DB Cooper-566, so you can open the page and read it yourself.

Each report comes with its sources: the Elsinore Ghost and Oscillations reports have companion PDFs reproducing every FBI page they cite, each page labeled with its Part, PDF page and Bates number.
