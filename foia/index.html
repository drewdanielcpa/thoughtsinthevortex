How the FOIA clock & privacy waivers work

Live "Days Since Submission" Counter: Under the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(6)(A)(i)), federal agencies have 20 business days (roughly 28 calendar days, excluding weekends and federal holidays) to make an initial determination on a request. Each card above calculates the exact elapsed calendar days and estimated business days in your browser every day until a final determination is logged.

Why Requests Are Split by Track: The FBI's Record/Information Dissemination Section (RIDS) routes requests by page volume and subject type. Narrow paper requests under 50 pages qualify for the Small Track queue, whereas requests for physical evidence envelopes (such as Bulky Exhibit LA 164-497-1B1) or Laboratory bench-note packets (SE 164A-81-1D890) require a manual Evidence Control Center or case-file pull. Deceased-individual requests attach official proof of death or birth records over 100 years old so RIDS analysts can lift Exemption (b)(6) and (b)(7)(C) personal-privacy redactions.
